# Inbox

One place to jot something down the moment it comes to mind, "Permission slip for Friday", before deciding whether it's an event, a task or a note. Sort it out later, when there's a minute.

The inbox is for grown-ups: it's on a parent's device, not on wall screens set to everyday access or kids' devices. A thought in the inbox isn't a chore, earns no points and doesn't notify anyone.

## Jot it down

Tap the **inbox** button at the top of the screen, beside the bell. It's there on every screen. Its small gray number is how many things are waiting (gray, not the bell's red: nothing in it is urgent).

Type, then **Save** or press Enter. The box clears and stays ready for the next one. There's no day, person, list or type to pick first. If saving doesn't work (no connection, say), what you typed goes back in the box. Something typed but not saved asks before the inbox closes.

You can also add to it from an AI assistant connected to Kinwall ("Add 'permission slip for Friday' to my inbox"). See [MCP](../integrations/mcp.md).

## Sort it out later

Tap a thought to sort it. What you wrote stays at the top, and you pick what it becomes:

* **A task on a list**: one of the family's to-do lists, with an optional due date. **Add task** adds it.
* **An event on the calendar**: pick the day, then **Next: event details** opens the usual new-event form for the time, who it's for and the calendar. **Add event** adds it, to Google or Outlook too when that calendar is synced.
* **A note on the family board**: shows on everyone's Home as a [family announcement](announcements.md) until the day you pick (three days with none).

A day word in what you wrote ("Friday", "tomorrow") fills in the next such day, and says so ("It says “Friday”, so this starts as Friday, Nov 20"). It's only a starting point: nothing is added until you tap the button, and you can change or clear the day. Day words are understood in English for now.

A thought leaves the inbox only once its task, event or note is added. **Later**, ✕ or closing the event form leaves it where it was. If adding fails, it stays in the inbox to try again. Tapping twice adds it once, and so does sorting the same thought from two phones at the same moment.

**Not needed** clears a thought without adding anything, with **Undo**. Nothing is deleted: sorted and cleared thoughts are listed under **Recently sorted**. Tap a sorted one to see where it went, or **Put back** to return a cleared one to the inbox.

The inbox is part of the family's [export and backups](../your-data/export-import.md). Turn it off in **Settings → General → Features → Inbox**.
