# Away days

**Away days** are whole days when someone isn't home: band camp, a weekend at Grandma's, a trip. On a person's away days their own chores pause, so nothing is missed, late or piled up, and their streak waits. A chore can instead stay due, or go to someone at home for those days and come back afterward.

Away days work with or without [Trips](trips.md), and keep working when Trips is turned off. Open them from **✈️ Away days** at the top of Chores, [Settings → Family → Away days](../settings/family.md#away-days), a trip's **Away days** row, or at `#/away`. Grown-ups set them up from their own devices; everyone sees who's away.

## Setting them up

**Add away days** (a grown-up) goes in three steps. Nothing is saved before the last one.

1. **Who and when.** A name ("Band camp"), then each person who's away with the day they leave and the day they're back. Each person can have their own dates (Sam joins on Wednesday). Two boxes, **Away on the day they leave** and **Away on the day they’re back**, say whether those days count as away; both are ticked to start. Untick one for an evening flight or a morning return. Leaving and coming back the same day with either box unticked means no away days, and the step says so.
2. **Chores.** Every one of the away person's own chores on those days, each with:
   * **Pause while away** (the default): not due on away days, and it never shows up late afterward.
   * **Keep due (they can do it away)**: for something they can do while away ("Read 20 minutes"). It counts as usual, streak too.
   * **Give to {name} while away**: someone at home does it for the dates you pick (within the away days). Only people home on all those days are offered. Schoolwork can be paused or kept due, but not handed to someone else.
3. **Review.** What's paused, handed over and kept due; anything to check first; and what keeps going. **Save away days** saves it all. If something blocks saving (a chore handed to someone who's away then, or one chore handed to two people on the same day), go back and change it.

From a trip with dates, **Set up** fills in the trip's name, dates and the people going.

The review is checked again when you save. If chores or other away days changed in the meantime, Kinwall asks you to look again rather than save something out of date.

## What pauses, and what keeps going

| On someone's away day | |
|---|---|
| Their own chores | Paused (unless kept due or handed over). Not in "N left", never late, no catch-up copies when they're back. |
| 🔥 Streak | Away days are skipped: they don't break or grow a streak and don't use a grace day. A long trip doesn't lose a streak from before it. |
| Weekly goals ("3 times a week") | A week that overlaps away days can't fail, but earns no free points and the target doesn't change. |
| "Just one of them" and Anyone chores | Carry on with whoever is home; they pause only when everyone who can do them is away. |
| Schoolwork | Paused like a day off, unless kept due. School calendars and records aren't changed; away is never logged as "No school". |
| Bus and pickup reminders | None for an away kid; a sibling at home still gets theirs. If the grown-up who usually drives or picks up is away while a kid is home, the review warns you to arrange the ride, and that grown-up gets no reminder for it while away. The usual school ride setting isn't changed. |
| Routine mode | Paused chores don't keep apps locked. Bedtime cutoffs stay. |
| Home upkeep | Due dates, records, supplies and notes to grown-ups carry on. An away kid's upkeep chore waits until they're back. |
| Medicines | Never paused. Reminders keep going. |
| Calendar events, meals, library dates | Unchanged. The review lists anything worth knowing. |
| Done work | Work already done, or waiting for an OK, keeps its points. |

## Handing a chore to someone at home

A handed-over chore shows on the recipient's list for those days, marked "From Maya until Tue". They can tick it, they get the points and the streak credit, and approvals work as usual. The owner is excused for those days but earns nothing for them. Afterward it goes back to its owner automatically; the chore itself is never edited, so its schedule, history and points stay as they were.

## While someone's away

* The Board's **Today at a glance** says "✈️ Alex, Maya and Leo away, back Sat".
* Chores shows "✈️ Maya is away until Sat. Their chores wait until they're back."
* An away kid's own device says "✈️ Enjoy your trip! Your usual chores wait until you're back." (and mentions any a grown-up kept due).
* The Chores leaderboard marks them ✈️ **Away**.

## Overlaps, changes and deleting

* Away days that overlap combine. Deleting one never cancels another.
* Open a set of away days to change it: back early, staying longer, someone else joining. The same review shows what changes.
* **Delete away days…** ends them from today on: today and the days ahead go back to normal. Days already gone stay excused, so streaks and history don't change.
* Changing a trip's dates never moves its away days by themselves; the trip shows them for you to check.
* Deleting a trip keeps its away days.
